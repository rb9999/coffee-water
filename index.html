Assumes 100 mg/mL stock solutions (10g salt dissolved in 100mL distilled water) of magnesium sulfate heptahydrate, calcium chloride, and potassium bicarbonate. Mg stock = magnesium sulfate stock. Ca stock = calcium chloride stock. Alk stock = potassium bicarbonate stock.

Making the stock solutions

Do this once per chemical (3 times total). Each batch makes a 100 mg/mL stock.

  1. Weigh 10g of the powder on a gram-accurate scale.
  2. Place it in a graduated container and add distilled water up to the 100mL mark (don't add 10g powder + 100mL water separately — the powder itself takes up some volume).
  3. Stir or shake until fully dissolved.
  4. Label the bottle (chemical name, "100mg/mL", date made).
  5. Repeat for the other two chemicals in separate containers.

To dose: add the calculator's mL amounts either by volume (dosing syringe) or by weight — since these are dilute solutions, 1mL ≈ 1–1.05g on a scale, so weighing the stock directly onto your water is a close and often easier substitute for measuring volume.
