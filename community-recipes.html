These are adapted from Barista Hustle's published DIY water recipes (baristahustle.com/diy-water-recipes-redux). Their recipes use zero calcium — all hardness comes from magnesium sulfate (Epsom salt) alone, paired with sodium bicarbonate for alkalinity. Magnesium targets here are converted from their calcium-carbonate-equivalent hardness figures to elemental magnesium (divided by 4.115) to match this calculator's units. Set your alkalinity source to baking soda to match the original recipes.
